CIFAR-10-C, a corruption robustness benchmark by Dan Hendrycks and Thomas Dietterich,
"Benchmarking Neural Network Robustness to Common Corruptions and Perturbations",
ICLR 2019. Distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Source: https://zenodo.org/record/2535967

This directory contains a subset: four (corruption, severity) blocks re-encoded as
lossless WebP sprite sheets for delivery to Google Colab. The pixel data is unmodified;
each block decodes byte-for-byte to the corresponding slice of the original .npy files.
Built by Deep_Learning/_workfiles/build_cifar10c_cache.py.
