Definitions
Attention mask
Specifies which source positions an attention query may use. An excluded position has attention weight zero.
Causal self-attention
The output at position t may use source positions 1 through t, including t itself. Later source positions are excluded.
Attention weights
Nonnegative coefficients that combine source values. For each query, weights sum to one over its permitted sources. With finite scores, softmax gives each permitted source positive weight.
Masked normalization
Exponentiate the scores, retain the permitted sources, and divide each retained term by their sum. Equivalently, restrict an unmasked softmax row and renormalize its retained weights.
