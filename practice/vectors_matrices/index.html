Shapes. The inner sizes must match. They drop out, and the result keeps the outer sizes.

    Show the full worked explanation
    1. Entry 1 of W x pairs row 1 of W with x: 1·2 + 0·(−1) + (−2)·1 = 2 + 0 − 2 = 0.
    2. Entry 2 pairs row 2 with x: 3·2 + 1·(−1) + 1·1 = 6 − 1 + 1 = 6.
    3. So W x = (0, 6): one entry per row of W. A 2 × 3 matrix turns a 3-vector into a 2-vector.
    4. For the shapes: (4 × 2)(2 × 5). The inner sizes, 2 and 2, match, so the product is defined. The outer sizes give the result: 4 × 5.
