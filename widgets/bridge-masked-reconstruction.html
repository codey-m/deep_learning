Masked reconstruction: how much to hide

A masked autoencoder hides patches and learns to predict them. Here a shortcut fills each hidden patch by extending the nearby visible pixels, with no idea what the object is.

How much must be hidden before that shortcut stops working?

What changedAt a low ratio, extending nearby pixels already fills the gaps, so the task can be solved without recognizing the object. At a high ratio it cannot. At a low ratio the error on all patches looks small because most patches are visible and copied, so judge a reconstruction by its error on the hidden patches.
