Practice: diagnose a VAE objective

Change one term at a time. Predict the direction first, then distinguish the minimized positive loss from the maximized ELBO convention.

positive beta-VAE loss = reconstruction + beta x KL = 45.0
standard-sign ELBO-style value = -reconstruction - KL = -45.0

Lower positive loss is preferred during minimization. Larger ELBO is preferred during maximization. When beta differs from 1, the beta-weighted training objective is not the standard ELBO.
