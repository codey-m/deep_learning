Weight symmetries: the same function, different weights

Two networks, A and B, compute the same function. B is A with its two hidden units swapped. Rescale a unit of A, or align B, and watch what happens to the weights, to the distance between them, and to their coordinatewise average.

Each unit computes r(z) = max(0, z), and the network outputs v1r(u1x) + v2r(u2x). Scaling one unit's incoming weight by a positive a and dividing its outgoing weight by the same a leaves the function alone, which is why A never moves in the plot however far its weights travel.
