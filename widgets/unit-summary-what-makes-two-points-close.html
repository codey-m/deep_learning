What makes two points close

An encoder places inputs, and training places whole networks. In each space, what decides which points end up near each other?

1 · Triplet

Triplet loss

In this example, the encoder places each image on a circle.

Training should put similar images close together and different images far apart.

Triplet loss checks one anchor, one positive and one negative at a time. The negative must be farther from the anchor than the positive is, by a margin.

2 · InfoNCE

InfoNCE

Triplet loss compares the anchor with one negative at a time.

InfoNCE compares the anchor with every image in the batch, through a softmax.

Cross-entropy then asks the softmax to put its probability on the positive.
